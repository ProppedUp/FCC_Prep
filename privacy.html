Privacy Policy

FCCPrep is an independent study tool designed to help users prepare for FCC commercial radio operator examinations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use FCCPrep.com and how that information is used.

Last Updated: June 2026

Information We Collect

FCCPrep does not require user accounts and does not ask users to provide personal information in order to use the study tools.

  • Pages visited
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Device and browser information
  • General site usage statistics

Analytics

FCCPrep uses Google Analytics to better understand how visitors use the website and to improve the study experience.

Cookies and Local Storage

FCCPrep may use cookies or local browser storage to save study sessions, test history, and site preferences on your device.

Data Sharing

FCCPrep does not sell personal information to third parties.
